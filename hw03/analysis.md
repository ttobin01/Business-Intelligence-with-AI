# HW3 Analysis: Do Executive Changes Precede or Follow Earnings?

Based on `corporate_events_timeline.csv`, executive changes at these five companies mostly preceded earnings announcements: 22 of 30 events (11 of 16 distinct 8-K filings) were filed before the nearest earnings filing, 6 events (4 filings) after, and 2 events (1 filing) in the same week. The largest changes followed this pattern: Walmart's CEO succession was filed 6 days before its Q3 FY26 release, Apple's CEO transition 10 days before its fiscal Q2 2026 results, and JPMorgan's elevation of two executives to Co-President 19 days before its Q2 2026 results, which is consistent with companies disclosing leadership news ahead of the earnings call so management can address it there. The pattern does vary by company: Apple, JPMorgan, NVIDIA and Walmart were mostly "before," while two of Microsoft's three events (board changes tied to its annual shareholder meeting) came after earnings. With only 16 filings, the lean toward "before" is suggestive rather than conclusive: a one-sided binomial test of 11 of 15 non-same-week filings against a 50/50 split gives p ≈ 0.06. Timing is also measured from 8-K filing dates rather than effective dates, and several changes take effect months later (e.g., Apple's CEO transition on 2026-09-01), so this analysis describes when changes are *disclosed*, not when they happen.
